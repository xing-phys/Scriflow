# Scriflow 0.44.0-alpha.2 — Reading layouts and tap navigation

- In **⋮ → Reading view**, choose **Continuous** or **Page turns** independently
  of **Single page** or **Double page**. Continuous pages can scroll vertically
  or horizontally; the direction is saved even while page turns are selected.
- In EPUB, MOBI and other reflowable books, PDFs and notes, tap the right or
  bottom half to advance; tap the upper-left quarter to go back. Double-page
  PDF and note views turn by a spread. Links, figures, text selection and stylus
  writing retain their own actions. Finger movement and fast ebook taps are
  handled more reliably.
- The final short page or spread can be brought into view in continuous mode.
  Saved ink remains attached to its original page and coordinates.

The host suite and 62 browser-based ebook checks pass. Android emulator checks
covered all twelve PDF/note layout and orientation combinations, rapid taps,
horizontal swipes, and GeckoView ebook taps in one and two columns in portrait
and landscape.

This prerelease includes the ebook contents, real two-column spreads and browser
sign-in popup fixes from alpha.1. Physical MatePad testing is still needed for
finger and stylus routing, e-ink rendering, reading layouts and X/Google sign-in.
Install the attached permanently signed APK over the existing app and use the
checksum file to verify it. VersionCode **120**. Notebook, ink, PDF and backup
formats are unchanged. Reading-view preferences use a new local version; older
app versions cannot read newly saved preferences. **0.33.2 remains Latest stable.**
